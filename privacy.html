Privacy Policy for Sagatama Mart

Last Updated: March 2026

At Sagatama Mart, accessible via Pi Browser, we prioritize the privacy of our users. This document outlines the types of information we collect and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect essential data required to fulfill your orders, such as your Name, Shipping Address, and Pi Wallet Address for transaction verification.

2. Pi Network SDK & Security

Sagatama Mart uses the official Pi Network SDK for all payments. We NEVER ask for, collect, or store your Pi Wallet Passphrase. All authentication and payment approvals happen securely within the Pi Network infrastructure.

3. Data Usage & Protection

Your information is used solely to process orders and communicate regarding delivery. We use secure backend services (Firebase) to ensure your data is protected and never sold to third parties.
